# Kynda LHM-71: a Tun beside a blast furnace raises its capacity, like the Skip does.
#
# The Tun's Stations default is "smelter,blastfurnace". A cfg saved before LHM-71 keeps "smelter"
# until the line is edited, and then this scenario fails at the first logged step, which is the
# thing to check first.
#
# BEFORE RUNNING, in the profile's ezomic.valheim.kynda.cfg: Verbose = true, and Stations under
# [Trough] set to smelter,blastfurnace. Config entries are read once at load, so restart after.
#
# NOT YET RUN. Stations reread their upgrades every three seconds, so each change is allowed four.
# The Tun gives its own figures (OreCapacity 20, FuelCapacity 40), so a blast furnace's 10 ore and
# 20 coal become 30 and 60, as a smelter's do.

clear
god
ghost
sweep

wipe 14
wait 1

flatten
wait 1

place blastfurnace 8 0
wait 2

place kynda_tun 8 3.6
wait 4
logged blastfurnace capacity ore 10 to 30, fuel 20 to 60 yes
hover kynda_tun Tun

break kynda_tun
wait 4
logged blastfurnace capacity ore 30 to 10, fuel 60 to 20 yes
