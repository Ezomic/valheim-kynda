# Kynda LHM-47 and LHM-71: the furnace upgrade raises a blast furnace's capacity and nothing else,
# and a Tun beside the same furnace adds nothing on top of it.
#
# A blast furnace holds 10 ore and 20 coal. With the furnace upgrade within range it holds 30 and
# 60 (OreCapacity 20 and FuelCapacity 40 are the defaults), and the stations reread their upgrades
# every three seconds, so each change is allowed four. Throughput is not on the list because the
# mod never writes m_secPerProduct or m_fuelPerProduct, and a scenario cannot time a smelt.
#
# BEFORE RUNNING, in the profile's ezomic.valheim.kynda.cfg: Verbose = true. The capacity change is
# only logged with it, and the assertions read that line. Config entries are read once at load, so
# restart the game after.
#
# NOT YET RUN, and the skip has never been seen in game either. What this proves is the wiring:
# the piece resolves a station through its own Stations list, counts only its own kind, and gives
# the capacity back when it is torn down. It says nothing about the model, the skin or the icon.
#
# `place` puts the pieces down for free, so no cost is paid and the build menu rule (the piece is
# absent until the character has held black metal) is not exercised here. The furnace is placed
# first and the upgrade 3.6 m to its side, inside Kynda's 4 m range. The Tun goes to the other
# side at the same distance. Since LHM-71 the Tun also serves a blast furnace, but one station gets one
# piece's figures: the Tun is declared first so it counts and the Skip reports it adds nothing.
# Both give 30 and 60, so no capacity line is logged until the last piece is gone.

clear
god
ghost
sweep

wipe 14
wait 1

flatten
wait 1

place blastfurnace 8 0
wait 2

place kynda_furnace 8 3.6
wait 4
logged blastfurnace capacity ore 10 to 30, fuel 20 to 60 yes
hover kynda_furnace Skip

place kynda_tun 8 -3.6
wait 4
hover kynda_tun Tun
hover kynda_furnace already_upgraded

break kynda_tun
wait 4
hover kynda_furnace Skip

break kynda_furnace
wait 4
logged blastfurnace capacity ore 30 to 10, fuel 60 to 20 yes
